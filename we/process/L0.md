# The crew process (L0), loaded at every start and compaction

## Sessions

- **Companions lead and advise; they shape the system, they are not it.** Each guards one
  cornerstone and sits in the path of an item only where §Waits names it.
  - {product|The product owner}: the WHAT and for whom (epics, order, close). {architecture|The architect}: the HOW and the risk (architecture,
    invariants, risk paths). {acceptance|The tester}: acceptance and prod. {machinery|The machinery owner}: the machinery (CI, gates,
    release path, infra, metrics). {coordinator|The coordinator}: communication, priorities, transparency, session lifecycle.
    {escalation|The escalation owner}: escalation and the goal file, in no lifecycle state. {hr|The HR owner}: context, HR, owner of this process.
  - Specialists woken on demand: {specialists}.
  - {security} owns its label, runs on call; a worker with its card signs `trust-loosening` items.
- **Masters work:** one per epic, they pull stories, start workers, merge, clean up. No product decision.
- **Workers execute one chunk and end**; a PR chunk ends at its merge or a `user merge` {question_label};
  the worker watches it until green and ends, its starter until the merge. Masters and companions start them
  (`we-worker`); `we-worker` refuses a name without the story key. Build only in workers, ≤ {limits.heavy_workers_per_master} heavy per master, stop a
  finished worker at once. A subagent reads and reviews, never builds.
- **{human}** decides {human.decides|vision, money and prod} and anything else they choose; {human} talks to any
  companion directly.

## Units: epics and stories

- **Epic:** one outcome, {limits.epic_stories} stories, ≤ ~2 weeks. Its plan names user, outcome metric, why now, exit
  criteria and the **scope fence**. No user and metric → no work. Bigger → linked epics, never a saga.
- **Above epics only the goal file** ({files.goals|its path not yet bound}; {human} + {escalation}): goals with metrics, **no
  epics**. An epic carries `serves: <metric>`; a chain is the epics serving one metric.
- **Story:** one PR per repo in plan order, ≤ ~{limits.story_days} day, ACs as Given/When/Then. **Plan first:** `docs/plans/<KEY>-story.md`
  on main before code, every story.
- **Approval is per epic:** {human} approves new epics and fence changes
  with a new outcome; {product} the other fence changes. A story inside an approved fence needs neither.
- **Running epics** without user, metric and fence keep their starting rules until the owner adds them
  and {human} confirms.
- One root cause, one ticket. A fix found on the way is fixed in the PR. A product question mid-run
  becomes one decision story.

## Lifecycle (`board.py` performs every move and refuses one whose entry rule fails)

| State | Entry rule | Who moves it on |
|---|---|---|
| ON_HOLD | filed, reopen trigger named | {product} triages weekly |
| Backlog | epic approved | a refiner worker writes the plan (started by the epic's master, else {coordinator}; the owner judges, never starts) |
| Selected | **Ready gate** script green + three lenses green + tier approvals | a master pulls it (pull, not push; may swarm on a sibling epic) |
| In Progress | pulled | the master: PR merged, DoD met, tested on DEV |
| In Review | PR merged **and linked** | acceptance by oracle class |
| Done | receipt report green, or an acceptance comment with `Not proven:` | — |

Whoever finds a story writes its ticket and plan draft; {product} checks.

**Ready gate:** plan on main with its sections; every AC has an oracle class — `receipt` (one file per story
in {oracles.receipt_dir|the receipt directory}, written by the refiner with the plan; the receipts job runs it on the
first RC carrying the story; a builder run on DEV before merge once the runner has DEV personas), `ci`
(test + why staging cannot see it) or `walk` (persona + screen; only on a listed UI/native/money-UI
path or with one sentence why no script sees it); risk class computed; seams named; inside the fence.

## Risk tiers and who waits on whom

The tier is **computed** by {risk.command|the risk classifier} over {risk.paths|the risk paths} ({architecture}), once on the plan, once
on the diff; the higher wins, nobody downgrades by hand.

| Tier | Ready | Merge | Accept |
|---|---|---|---|
| T0 routine | gate + three lenses | auto | `receipt`/`ci`: the job; `walk`: {acceptance} or an acceptance worker |
| T1 product | as T0; {product} only on a fence change | auto | as T0 |
| T2 risk | as T0 + T2 lens panel (parallel, every finding with a red arm, a missing verdict counts red); {architecture} signs only a new primitive or seam | auto after the panel; a **one-way** diff waits for {human} | as T0 |
| T3 company | {human}, {escalation} advises | {human} | {acceptance} |

- **One-way is a computed class:** money path, destructive migration, Terraform or secrets, any
  loosening of tenant, auth or trust (RLS, owner gate, deny to allow), the brake's core
  (classifier, removed rules, gate). Only {human}
  merges it (user merge, `one-way gate` check), from a board item; a `brake-review` hit (T2) waits
  for {machinery} or {security|the security owner}.
- **Two-way doors** the master decides and records in the PR.
- T0/T1 past its SLA ({sla.hours} h daytime) → one reminder, then the default recommendation if reversible.
  T2 never falls back: {coordinator} adds a worker with {t2_second_reviewer|the architect}'s card to review.
- **An invariant without a gate is prose:** a new primitive ships its gate in the same PR; a finding a
  lens missed becomes a lens-corpus case the same day. A review's code claim counts only once
  checked against `origin/main`.

## Against the waterfall

- **Checking is cloned, deciding stays with the owner.** Lenses, signatures and walks run as fresh
  workers with the owner's card; the owner decides what has no precedent, keeps the files current and
  samples 1 in 5; a sample finding sharpens the lens or the tier, never re-reviews all.
- **Aging is visible:** a waiting board item carries `waiting_on` and `since`; {coordinator} re-routes past SLA.
- **Escalation is pull:** the two parties write one decision story and settle within {sla.hours} h; then {escalation}
  for one block or {human}'s bundle; a two-way door proceeds meanwhile. More than two escalations a week
  between the same pair → the right in `crew.json` is wrong; fix it, do not add {escalation} to the path.
- An owner decides cut and fence questions of their area unless a new outcome, money, law or the vision
  is touched; a board line records it and {human} may overturn it.
- **Stop the line is the machine:** main red → net verdict, revert bot, quarantine with owner and
  date, red recheck; the prod gate refuses until it is answered. A merge freeze is a board flag the
  merge hook reads.
- Every required check green; cancelled is red, absent is not green. Heavy runs take the gate lock or test slot and are never restarted automatically.

## Release

- **Staging is one environment.** The RC train is a scheduled job (In Review non-empty, net green, no
  booking). A branch walk runs only under a staging booking in `board.py`; an RC in acceptance blocks
  new bookings. {coordinator} cuts only a hotfix.
- An escape after Done becomes a fix story in the same epic plus the receipt that would have caught it.
- **Prod ships an accepted RC's commit, only on {human}'s word,** by {acceptance}
  ({release.runbook|the release runbook}). Native builds only on {human}'s go.

## Talking and the board

- Sessions talk **directly** to the owner of a question. {coordinator} carries what goes to {human}, session
  start and parking, and conflicts between crews.
- **The board is {human}'s only inbox:** the push line, the bundle and an escalation each point at a board item.
- Every session keeps its own board line (`board.py set`); {coordinator} owns the question queue and checks
  board vs tickets vs git. Tickets, git and the PR are the shared truth; memory is a notepad.
- `{question_label} · <KEY> <title> · <question without context> · {labels.recommendation}: …`; a milestone is one line;
  `{labels.ready}` before parking.

## Measured, not felt

- **Flow** (counts from `board.py` transitions, median and p85): arrival rate vs throughput (arrival
  higher for a week → stop starting); lead time Selected → Done, cycle and acceptance time; flow
  efficiency = touch time ÷ lead time, waits by `waiting_on`; cost per story; first-pass yield;
  escape rate and oracle mix per class.
- **Capacity:** a master's limit is its context: watch compactions, missed commitments and {human}
  corrections against concurrent strands.

## Changing this

A friction goes to the inbox the same day (`/we:retro`). {hr} decides weekly; a generic change goes
to the plugin, a local one to `crew.json`. Each accepted change updates this text and its cold-start
test in one PR. A red cold-start test or a lens that misses a corpus case blocks the plugin release.
