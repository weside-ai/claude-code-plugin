# The crew process (L0)

## Flow first

Outranks every other line, role cards included.

- **No sham security:** no lock, gate or hook against an imagined rogue session.
- **Convention and permissions** (`ask`/`deny` catch mistakes) carry daily work.
- **Enforced only where an accident hurts prod** (prod data, release, secrets, deletion, money, native builds,
  one-way classes); a brake counts its stops and goes when most had no danger.
- **Flow before security:** {coordinator} is the flow master; panels, cards and lenses are input, not gates. In doubt, flow wins.
- A new control names the prod or accident damage it catches.

## Sessions

- **Companions lead and advise:** each guards one cornerstone, in an item's path only where §Risk
  tiers says.
  - {product|The product owner}: the WHAT and for whom (epics, order, close). {architecture|The architect}: the HOW and the risk (architecture,
    invariants, risk paths). {acceptance|The tester}: acceptance and prod. {machinery|The machinery owner}: the machinery (CI, gates,
    release path, infra, metrics). {coordinator|The coordinator}: communication, priorities, transparency, session lifecycle.
    {escalation|The escalation owner}: escalation and the goal file, in no lifecycle state. {hr|The HR owner}: context, HR, owner of this process.
  - Specialists, started at once by whoever needs them (`references/companion-session.md`): {specialists}.
  - {security} owns its label, runs on call; a worker with its card reviews each card hit.
- **Masters work:** one per epic, they pull stories, start workers, merge, clean up. No product decision.
- **Workers execute one chunk and end**; a PR chunk ends at its merge or a `user merge` {question_label};
  the worker watches to green, its starter to the merge or its event line. Masters and companions start them
  (`we-worker <KEY>-…`) and stop a finished one at once (`we-worker stop <name>`); build only in workers,
  ≤ {limits.heavy_workers_per_master} heavy per master. A subagent reads and reviews, never builds.
- **{human}** decides {human.decides|vision, money and prod} and whatever else they choose, and talks to any companion directly.{single}
- **{me}** decides {me.decides} on this machine; other humans and their states: your role card.

## Units: epics and stories

- **Epic:** one outcome, {limits.epic_stories} stories, ≤ ~2 weeks. Its plan names user, metric, why now, exit
  criteria and the **scope fence**. No user and metric → no work.
- **Above epics only the goal file** ({files.goals|its path not yet bound}; {human} + {escalation}): goals with metrics, **no
  epics**. An epic carries `serves: <metric>`.
- **Story:** one PR per repo in plan order, ≤ ~{limits.story_days} day, ACs as Given/When/Then. **Plan first:** `docs/plans/<KEY>-story.md`
  on main before code.
- **Fast lane, the default for small work** off every risk path: no plan file, refiner, panel or
  Selected. The master pulls it (`board.py move <KEY> in_progress --fast`); one worker writes the ACs
  with oracle class and `Not proven:` into the PR body and builds; the PR's diff computes the class.
- **Approval per epic, never per story:** {human} approves it with `line:` (≤ ~8 keys, a
  measurable goal); {product} the rest of the fence. Free inside: a sharper AC, an AC's defect, small work at
  the touched place. A new story, behaviour or place goes unbuilt to `## Extensions`; {product} pre-sorts,
  {human} decides each in the bundle.
- **Last line story Done** → the master asks "good as is?" on the board and tells
  {coordinator} "finish line reached"; no building until answered.
- One root cause, one ticket; a product question mid-run is one decision story.

## Lifecycle (`board.py` refuses a failed entry rule)

| State | Entry rule | Who moves it on |
|---|---|---|
| ON_HOLD | filed, reopen trigger named | {product} triages weekly |
| Backlog | epic approved, key on its `line:` | a refiner worker writes the plan (the epic's master starts it, else {coordinator}; the owner judges) |
| Selected | **Ready gate** green, tier computed; on a T2 plan three lenses green | a master pulls it (pull, not push; may swarm on a sibling epic) |
| In Progress | pulled from Selected, or `--fast` | the master: PR merged, DoD met |
| In Review | PR merged **and linked** | acceptance by oracle class |
| Done | receipt report green, or an acceptance comment with `Not proven:` | — |

**Ready gate:** plan on main with its sections; every AC has an oracle class — `receipt` (a file per story
in {oracles.receipt_dir|the receipt directory}, written with the plan, run on the first RC carrying it), `ci`
(a running test + why staging cannot see it) or `walk` (persona + screen; only on a listed walk
path or with one sentence why no script sees it); risk class computed; seams named; inside the fence.

## Risk tiers and who waits on whom

{risk.command|`risk_paths.py`} over `.weside/risk-paths` gives T0 or T2: on a PR's diff, on the plan at `select` with a crew `risk.command`. No script computes T1/T3; they name who waits ({product}, {human}); none is lowered by hand.

| Tier | Ready | Merge | Accept |
|---|---|---|---|
| T0 routine | gate (fast lane: none) | auto | `receipt`/`ci`: the job; `walk`: {acceptance} or an acceptance worker |
| T1 product | as T0; {product} only on a fence change | auto | as T0 |
| T2 risk | gate + three lenses (a plan go never replaces the Ready panel); {architecture} signs only a new primitive or seam | auto after the required checks; **one-way** below | as T0 |
| T3 company | {human}, {escalation} advises | {human} | {acceptance} |

- **One-way is a computed class:** computed amounts, destructive migration, Terraform or secrets, the
  brake core (classifier, gates, review workflows, removed rules, crew `risk`). A one-way PR merges
  on {human}'s board yes: the master asks it on the board (`board.py ask --cause merge-go --pr <PR>
  --one-way <class>`), then arms with `arm_pr.sh`. `--admin` only on {human}'s go.
- T0/T1 past its SLA ({sla.hours} h daytime) → one reminder, then the default recommendation if reversible,
  at once for a parked owner. T2 never falls back: {coordinator} adds a worker with {t2_second_reviewer|the architect}'s card to review.
- A lens's miss becomes a lens-corpus case. Code claims count once checked on
  `origin/main`; a question quotes `path:line` and {human}'s words.

## Against the waterfall

- **Checking is cloned, deciding stays with the owner.** Lenses, signatures and walks run as fresh
  workers with the owner's card; the owner decides what has no precedent and keeps the files current.
- {coordinator} re-routes a board wait (`waiting_on`) past SLA.
- **Escalation is pull:** both parties open one decision story (`docs/board.md` § Escalations), settle in
  {sla.hours} h, then {escalation} or {human}'s bundle; a two-way door proceeds meanwhile.
- An owner decides cut and fence questions of their area unless a new outcome, money, law or the vision
  is touched; a board line records it and {human} may overturn it.
- **Main red does not stop the line:** a PR merges on green required checks (cancelled is red,
  absent not green); one fix PR answers the red run; only prod waits.

## Release

- **Staging is one environment.** The RC train is the repo's scheduled job (In Review non-empty, no
  booking), else cut by hand. A branch walk runs only under a staging booking; an RC in acceptance blocks
  new bookings. {coordinator} cuts only a hotfix.
- An escape after Done becomes a fix story in the same epic; on a risk path it brings the receipt that would catch it.
- **Prod ships an accepted RC's commit, only on {human}'s word,** by {acceptance}
  ({release.runbook|the release runbook}). Native builds only on {human}'s go.

## Talking and the board

- Sessions talk **directly** to the owner of a question. {coordinator} carries only session start and
  parking, crew conflicts and undeliverable answers.
- **The board is {human.local}'s only inbox:** the push line, the bundle and an escalation each point at a board item.
- Each session keeps its line (`board.py set state=… now=…`) and asks its question:
  `board.py ask --key K --key-title T --ask Q --recommendation R --cause C`.
  The answer comes back to the asker (a worker asks its starter); on **Follow-up** {coordinator}
  only tells {human.local} whose window to go to.
  Tickets, git and PR are the truth.
- `{question_label} · <KEY> <title> · <question without context> · {labels.recommendation}: …`; a milestone is one line;
  `{labels.ready}` to {coordinator}, then `state=parked`. A follow-up names a checkable trigger, not a date.
- Never repeat an answered question (`board.py show <Q>`).
